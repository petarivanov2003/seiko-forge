Frequently Asked Questions

Are these authentic Seiko watches?

Our watches are custom-built modifications (mods). We use authentic, highly reliable Seiko automatic movements (like the NH35) housed in custom aftermarket parts.

How long does shipping take?

Because of the intricate assembly, rigorous quality testing, and total shipping turnaround, please allow 21 business days for your unique watch to be crafted and delivered to your door.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes! Every Seiko Forge watch comes with a comprehensive 1-year warranty covering any mechanical defects in the Seiko automatic movement and custom assembly.
